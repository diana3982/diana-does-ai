# experiments

Early prototypes, kept as history.

These are the first versions of Columba — the ones where the idea was still
being worked out. They are **not live code**, nothing in `backend/` or
`frontend/` imports them, and they don't reflect the app as it stands now.

They're here on purpose. It's worth being able to see how the idea evolved:
a single system prompt in `hello_world.py`, then a companion with a
personality in `cool_gal.py`, then character configs and quirks in
`companion.py` / `companion_v2.py`.

Read them as a record, not as documentation. In particular, the early prompts
scope the app to teenagers and tell users to reach out to "a trusted adult" —
Columba is for **anyone** who's struggling, at any age, and the live prompt in
`backend/companion.py` reflects that. The narrowing was the thing that got
fixed, and leaving it visible is the point.
